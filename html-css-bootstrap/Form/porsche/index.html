Features of CAR

Rear-Engine Layout

Placed uniquely over the rear axle to maximize traction and lower the center of gravity.

3.0L Flat-Six Twin-Turbo

Racetrack-proven engine pushing 388 horsepower and 450 Nm of torque.

8-Speed PDK Transmission

Dual-clutch gearbox engineered for rapid gear changes with zero loss of power.

Rapid Acceleration

Blasts from 0 to 100 km/h in 4.1 seconds with a top speed of 294 km/h.
